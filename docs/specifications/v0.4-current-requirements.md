## Product scope

WBM is a Home Assistant (HA) integration for explicitly authorized EV charging
and private household PV optimization. Each entry independently binds one
station, EVSE and connector and owns its listener, observations and settings.
Multiple entries need distinct ports. Each controller can see another EV's load
in household measurements. Coordinated multi-EV allocation and fairness are
outside this scope. [V04-01/02/05/11](passive-v0.4.md)

WBM serves the Stationary interface over the OCPP 2.1 WebSocket subprotocol
`ocpp2.1`. Configure the station path as `ws://HA-address:port/station-id`.
The supported scope is the implemented messages, scopes and Stationary device
model, with interoperability dependent on that contract. The station ID routes
connections. The approved trust boundary is the operator's private LAN with
authenticated VPN access and a commissioning check of listener exposure. The
default bind is `0.0.0.0:9000`; within that boundary plain WebSockets are
permitted. [Approved LAN decision](architecture-responsibility.md#approved-ocpp-lan-trust-boundary)

## Architectural responsibility

Stationary owns physical and electrical protection, CP, interlocks, phase
switching and actual current limits. WBM owns optimization, operator intent,
OCPP policy, scoped proposals and truthful presentation. Keep requested
current/phase, protocol acknowledgement, confirmed station offer, and measured
EV consumption distinct.

Ordinary observations are last-value-held within their applicable generation.
Silence and failed reads leave observations unchanged. Explicit invalidation,
disconnect and a new connection or boot are distinct events. Each supported
field initializes from its own scoped evidence. WBM uses the single canonical
store, with no redundant freshness manager, per-request physical proof or
parallel safety controller. Protocol deadlines and business timers remain
meaningful. [WBM-ARCH-01–12](architecture-responsibility.md)

## Authority, hardware permission and charge release

- **ControlAuthority** is the station's Local or Remote/OCPP current and phase
  authority. Only the operator's Take Control action requests Remote. Stationary
  owns the return to Local.
- **ChargingEnabled** is observed station hardware permission. Its separate HA
  switch works under either authority with a supported writable endpoint and
  confirmation. Enabling under Local may activate the station's own target.
- **Charge release** is manager-owned per-connector authorization. A profile
  selection prepares policy, and a real change retires release. Confirmed
  Remote-to-Local loss and reload also retire release. Start and Apply require
  observed current-generation hardware permission. Preparatory Start can
  authorize operation before an eligible transaction exists.
- **Transaction eligibility** and the selected profile's gates determine
  execution. Release OFF fences positive proposals and reconciles a confirmed
  retained positive offer to 0 A when Remote control and the valid transaction
  allow it. A confirmed zero requires no repeat. With hardware disabled or
  unknown, Stop can still revoke active release without approving drafts.
- The explicit `wallbox_manager.charge_action` service fences Start, Apply and
  Stop by profile, revision and runtime incarnation. Generic `button.press`
  retains the state-dependent action.

## NETZ profile

NETZ is event-driven and independent of PV inputs. Its configurable nominal
power uses 230 V per phase and rounded 0.1 kW offers. Only supported phase
modes with eligible per-mode caps contribute. A cap of 0 A disables the mode.
The backend resolves DOWN, NEAREST or UP and supplies legal step navigation
within each phase series and across its endpoints. A nominal point may differ
from measured charging power.

Draft power, phase hint and timing are separate from approved settings. Start
durably approves and arms a one-shot schedule. Apply commits meaningful edits
to the active schedule. Equivalent power edits follow the approved backend
equivalence rule. Stop retires release and pending positive intent. Delay and
duration use absolute business deadlines independent of vehicle connection or
power. A changed approved delay establishes a new epoch; a duration-only edit
retains the planned start. Focused editors share backend ownership with a
60-second lease and a 15-second post-edit Apply window for timing drafts.
Schedule expiry during editing preserves drafts for the next explicit Start.

An existing schedule can survive a transport gap, while old wire intent is
fenced and deadlines continue. Same-ID resume reconciles a matching readback
without rewriting, corrects a valid mismatch, and stays unconfirmed on
insufficient evidence. A genuinely new transaction, explicit action or an
unused delayed business window has its own eligibility. Reload retires release
and the live schedule while keeping durable settings. Recognized phase and
re-enable lockouts use the NETZ retry and interim-point rules. Planned battery
reserve is a read-only preview. Actual NETZ charging may own a temporary reserve
override with durable compare-before-restore recovery. Missing battery mappings
disable that feature only.

## PV profiles

| Profile | Current target and input contract |
| --- | --- |
| `PV_SURPLUS`, battery-free | With no mapped actual battery SoC, uses selected EV power and grid import/export. Target EV power is actual EV power plus export minus import. No battery target, separate PV/load source or forecast is required. |
| `PV_SURPLUS`, battery-equipped | Uses the configured `soll_soc_speicher` within reserve/hysteresis bounds. An out-of-range fixed target becomes unavailable. |
| `PV_OPTIMUM` | Uses a local-day planner, configured target bounds and estimated daily household consumption, mapped battery capacity and remaining-day PV energy. First observed surplus can enter dynamic planning. The forecast versus remaining household energy determines the end of the dynamic phase. |
| `PV_MAXIMUM` | Uses the lowest permitted battery target, mapped reserve plus hysteresis. Forecast is unnecessary. |

The battery path uses mapped reserve and actual SoC, grid import/export and
selected EV power for regulation. BALANCE requires both storage power channels.
PV production and total household power provide strict surplus evidence where
required. Household load excluding the selected EV is also used for the FAST
restart branch. Those conditional inputs need no fabricated defaults. Explicit
unknown, unavailable or invalid required inputs inhibit ordinary regulation
that needs them. Silence alone leaves valid values held. A missing mapping for
one profile does not authorize another profile or imply zero input.

The shared SoC matrix uses target T and hysteresis H. STOP below T−H is strict,
FAST enters at T+H inclusive, and STOP→BALANCE requires SoC strictly above
T+H/2 plus surplus evidence. The same scheduler computes canonical T and
PV_OPTIMUM day planning with release OFF or OCPP offline. Positive regulation
requires current operator and execution eligibility. PV offers Start/Stop.
An automatic 0 A pause retains release.

FAST leads with signed grid feedback and bounded upward exploration. BALANCE
leads with signed storage flow, target-SoC bias and economic fallback. Two
positive Fronius storage channels normalize to neutral for that snapshot.
There is no extra BALANCE smoothing. Both modes use legal phase-series steps,
EV uptake checks, shared startup settling and configured rise/fall timing.
Mandatory SoC/economic stops bypass ordinary settling. During a recognized
atomic PV phase lockout, an eligible confirmed point can continue with
same-phase adjustment or mandatory Stop. An ordinary eligible tick makes a
new phase decision after the wait.

## PV economics

The import budget is private optimization, with a configurable default of
50 Wh. WBM accounts prospectively from measured grid import during eligible
current-transaction intervals. Above half the applicable minimum-point power
it counts the full measured import over the supported interval. Economic STOP
requests zero at the configured budget. Sampling and station delays limit the
accuracy of the estimated intervention. No billing-grade claim applies.

Reset qualification needs **strictly more than 15 seconds** of continuously
supported import at or below the comparison threshold. Permission and input
gaps interrupt interval evidence. Restart additionally needs confirmed zero
offer and zero measured EV power, current authorization, eligibility and SoC
policy. Battery paths require positive signed storage charge, or in FAST
measured household load strictly below the configured limit. Battery-free
SURPLUS uses measured export greater than import. Current station lockouts
still apply.

Valid persisted Wh and STOP provenance survive a same-ID reconnect. A new
valid current-generation transaction on the same binding gets a fresh budget
on the first otherwise eligible cycle. A matching prior `Ended` is optional.
Damaged or unreconciled history inhibits automatic positive PV proposals.
Downtime and unqualified intervals do not advance the reset clock. The policy
uses measured evidence without the historical D budget, smoothing or extra
anti-restart latch.

## OCPP and session lifecycle

The listener routes by exact configured station and subprotocol. A replacement
connection and BootNotification fence older work. Bootstrap after registration
requests the supported device inventory and relevant readbacks. Inventory and
its unique supported endpoints describe capability, while valid Actual reads
and correctly scoped reports supply canonical state. Some measurements depend
on station-originated reports. Commands and outcomes are fenced by adapter,
generation, scope and intent, and by transaction where applicable. Supported
composite readback reconciles offer certainty separately from acceptance and
measured consumption.

Session attribution uses the external transaction ID, bound scope, sequence
and time, and a stable WBM session ID. An Updated event can establish an ongoing
session without inventing a start. Delayed or duplicate events and closed IDs
cannot replace a valid current record. Import-register measurements are distinct
from per-session kWh. Only eligible scoped and linked meter evidence contributes.
Boundary estimates carry provenance. Restored sessions must be reconfirmed by
valid current-generation transaction evidence before live attribution or
transaction control. The complete persisted closed-ID set prevents replay even
across repeated restarts.

## Persistence and restart

All keys use `wallbox_manager.<entry_id>` and HA Store wrapper version 1.

| Store | Current contract |
| --- | --- |
| `.profiles` | Settings keyed by JSON station/EVSE/connector tuple. Contains editable and approved NETZ fields, profile and scoped settings. Unknown unrelated stored values are preserved and inert. Central regulation options are authoritative where present. Startup begins with release OFF. NETZ initializes an absent/invalid nominal draft only after usable capability discovery. |
| `.battery` | Entry-owned temporary reserve record with entity, original and temporary values and optional pending change. Persists ownership before a write, checks observed values before restoration and preserves external edits. Missing/failed readback or storage is handled through the battery recovery/error path. |
| `.pv` | Payload version 1 with economics, day, station identity and Ended marker. A truly absent store starts with an unbound budget and initial planner. Unreadable, quarantined, malformed or unreconciled economic history inhibits automatic positive proposals. Serialized persistence and readback protect the eligible positive path. A write failure does not block a required zero proposal. |
| `.passive_session` | Payload schema 2 with the current/last session snapshot and complete sorted `closed_transactions`. New snapshots omit the live session generation and retain separate energy linkage metadata. Both schema-2 variants load with live generation unset. Unsupported payload schemas fail entry setup. `Store.async_load()` returning `None` currently starts without recovered replay history. |

Version 3 entries require explicit binding. Binding/registry conflicts and
historical subentry layouts fail before registry mutation. Legacy config
entry conversion and RestoreEntity adoption are outside the supported path. Retired
`.pv_day`, `.pv_economy` and `.sessions` formats are ignored, and earlier
session payload schemas are unsupported. Reload releases live intent and
reinitializes OCPP observations. On teardown, runtime resources are fenced
and closed even when a final journal write fails. The session Store's ambiguous
`None` result remains [OPEN](../v0.4/requirements-consolidation-review.md#open-session-journal-recovery).

## User interface and diagnostics

The entry-scoped Lovelace card uses backend profile-dependent editors and
Start/Apply/Stop state. NETZ shows delay/duration countdowns, editable drafts
and read-only planned reserve with observed reserve labeled separately.
Battery profiles display actual SoC and effective target when available.
The PV Target SoC sensor exposes the same canonical T without extra acquisition or
unchanged-value writes. Battery percentages and session energy use one locale
decimal. Actual power and directional grid (NETZ) or storage (PV) use kW with
two locale decimals. Neutral/invalid direction displays `—`. The card shows
confirmed and pending operating points separately and presents the latest
active warning or error. German and English follow HA language conventions.

HA publication follows meaningful changes in canonical state. Timing countdowns
render in the browser. A confirmed active session duration uses its own 60-second HA publication cadence. Diagnostic
levels cover errors at 0, lifecycle/failures at 1, relevant NETZ/OCPP context
and PV decisions at 2, and supported detailed OCPP read events at 3. Level 2
PV logs retain changed decisions and the approved compact unchanged eligible
cycle sample, with pause context and operating-point execution events.
Diagnostic logs are separate from Recorder entity publication.

## Validation and acceptance

- **TESTED:** unit and regression behavior, simulated OCPP exchanges and
  reconnects, fixture-based Stationary contract, HA entities/persistence,
  frontend regressions and headless Chrome layout. Static validation covers
  lint, format, JavaScript syntax, metadata, localization and documentation.
- **OPEN physical acceptance:** Stationary bootstrap and device discovery,
  authority and hardware permission, real NETZ Start/Apply/Stop, phases and
  current, EV reconnect and transaction rollover, PV FAST/BALANCE and economic
  STOP/restart, actual EV uptake, Fronius/BYD mapping and source timing, reserve
  recovery, trusted-LAN/VPN exposure and sustained HA/Recorder performance.
  V04-12 retains the passive HA CPU target of at most 0.1 percentage points
  average increase, requiring longer alternating live measurements.
- The [evidence map and open decisions](../v0.4/requirements-consolidation-review.md)
  distinguish coverage, deviations and acceptance gaps. Published beta evidence
  applies to the tagged release it names.
