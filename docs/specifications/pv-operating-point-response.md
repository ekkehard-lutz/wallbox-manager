# Shared PV operating-point navigation and EV settling

**Decision record:** 25 October 2026, operator's FAST discussion with explicit applicability to **both FAST and BALANCE**. **Status:** agreed FAST/BALANCE response and adjustable regulation parameters. Follow-up decision: the economic import-budget STOP overrides the 0 A startup hold; grid correction deadband defaults to ±100 W. Overall V3 remains DRAFT; documentation only. Overall PV V3 remains DRAFT; documentation only.

IDs: PV-STEP-01–06, PV-RESP-01–08, PV-TUNE-01–10.

## Common operating points (FAST and BALANCE)

| ID | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| **PV-STEP-01** | Both FAST and BALANCE use the **same** capability-driven legal operating-point model and technical current lattice as NETZ (WBM-GRID-31–39). Plan nominal power as `P_nominal_W = 230 V × phases × requested_A` without consuming live voltage measurements for requested-point identity. Retain actual measured EV power separately. Stationary remains sole physical/electrical safety authority. Only modes genuinely advertised and enabled via WBM current caps exist; 2p is optional when advertised, not assumed for Stationary. |
| **PV-STEP-02** | On **upward regulation**, use a **single next legal ampere/current step in the currently confirmed positive phase mode** no faster than the configured **`pv_increase_interval_cycles` (default: 3) eligible PV regulation cycles**. Exhaust that phase series to its allowed maximum **before** switching to the nearest strictly higher legal nominal point on another enabled series. A globally closer point in another overlapping phase mode must not cause an earlier switch. |
| **PV-STEP-03** | On **downward regulation**, stay in the currently confirmed positive phase mode for as long as an appropriate lower point exists there, even when a numerically closer point exists in an overlapping phase mode. When that phase series is exhausted at its minimum, change to the nearest strictly lower feasible point in another enabled series only if the desired lower power actually justifies leaving the current minimum. Large genuine reductions **may skip multiple current steps within the existing phase series in one regulation decision**, rather than spending a full cycle per 1 A, but must not use alternate overlapping phases for a finer approximation. If no positive point is economically warranted, the shared STOP/0 A policy applies. |
| **PV-STEP-04** | Illustrative Stationary ranges with 230 V planning, minimum 6 A, 1p cap 20 A, 3p cap 27 A: in 3p, 6 A is **4,140 W** and 7 A is **4,830 W**; disregard 1p/19 A **4,370 W** and 1p/20 A **4,600 W** while regulating within 3p. From 3p/6 A, a mere ideal point between 3,910 and 4,140 W does **not** prompt a premature 1p switch; only a truly lower point of **1p/17 A = 3,910 W** or less justifies the cross-series step. Conversely, 1p/20 A 4,600 W upward goes to 3p/7 A 4,830 W, not the lower 3p/6 A. These are examples, not hardcoded bounds or assumed 2p support. |
| **PV-STEP-05** | The common phase-first navigation semantics should **reuse** the existing tested NETZ technical model / `Envelope.navigate()` concepts, not be independently reproduced in FAST, BALANCE, or Lovelace. If a numerical power target is resolved via a different solver interface, it must still observe the **phase-first** precedence. Report the distinct **desired**, **requested**, **OCPP accepted**, **reported applied**, and **actually consumed** quantities faithfully. Phase-switch lockouts/rejection/recognized retry policy and owner protections remain separate from step selection. |
| **PV-STEP-06** | Interpret **`pv_increase_interval_cycles` ticks** (default 3) as configured, eligible PV regulation cycles measured relative to the previous upward change; for the current default 5 s interval this is nominally **15 s**, not a fixed new wall-clock 15 s constant. Never queue several successive upward points while one current/phase change remains unresolved. A downward correction is not limited to every third tick. Inactive/no-authority/passive operation creates no background ramp; a disconnect does not authorize phantom applied points. Exactly when the 3-tick counter starts relative to asynchronous OCPP acceptance/confirmation must preserve the guarded execution boundary and be tested; it is not a per-measurement-generation gate. |

## Shared EV consumption and settling (FAST and BALANCE)

| ID | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| **PV-RESP-01** | For any requested positive point, calculate offered **nominal** power from `230 V × number_of_phases × requested_current_A`. Compare with **actual total measured EV charging power** from WBM's canonical OCPP observations. The decision threshold is **at least 50%** of this nominal requested point: `P_EV_measured >= 0.5 × P_requested_nominal`. Do not use measured grid voltage to redefine the threshold; do not equate OCPP Accepted or reported amperes with consumed EV power. A valid 0 W is different from unknown/unavailable. The threshold is inapplicable to a requested 0 A point. |
| **PV-RESP-02** | **Upward adjustment is prohibited** while the vehicle consumes **less than 50%** of the currently offered nominal power, whether caused by initial EV startup, a temporary plug-release/unlock interval (~30 s), cell balancing or any other underconsumption. Upward adjustment may resume after actual consumption again reaches the threshold and the configured upward-cycle interval/one-outstanding-change rules permit it. A vehicle drawing less than offered power must never be interpreted as spare energy that justifies repeatedly raising the request. |
| **PV-RESP-03** | **Starting from 0 A is exceptional:** after a genuinely requested and executable `0 A → positive` transition, hold the initially selected positive operating point for **`pv_startup_hold_cycles` eligible regulation cycles (default 10)** with **no ordinary downward re-optimization**, even if the measured 50% threshold is reached earlier. This is a deliberately tolerated uncertain EV startup response, not an OCPP retry timer or a guaranteed physical charging start. Count active cycles of the same control context; do not manufacture elapsed cycles while permission, intent or command execution was never established. If the vehicle has not reached 50% after the configured startup hold, normal downward optimization may proceed on subsequent ticks, including down to 0 A where separately legitimate; upward optimization remains barred until 50% is measured. The complete configured startup hold applies to **both ordinary upward and downward optimization**, regardless of whether 50% of nominal offered power was reached early. |
| **PV-RESP-04** | During the configured startup hold, no ordinary *automatic point adjustment* is assumed. The shared three-tick upward cadence and 50% condition remain prerequisites afterward. The operator's explicit requirement to wait **all configured startup ticks in every case** applies to ordinary upward optimization too, even if measured consumption has already reached 50%; afterward, three-tick ramp timing and the 50% observation must both hold. |
| **PV-RESP-05** | **During ongoing charging with an already positive requested point**, the same 50% gate prevents further upward regulation after the EV temporarily reduces its demand (e.g. vehicle unlocked for unplugging or cell balancing). **Downward** optimization remains permitted when the current economic/SoC/grid conditions require it, independently of the EV's low consumption; it is subject only to the configured **`pv_decrease_delay_cycles`** (default **0**, meaning no added delay, decision on the current regular PV regulation tick), except that the startup hold and independently overriding economic/SoC/intent STOPs keep their precedence. A temporarily measured 0 W while a positive point remains selected is **not** a new `0 A → positive` startup and does not reset the 10-cycle hold. |
| **PV-RESP-06** | The startup ten-cycle and EV-underconsumption controls are **optimization delay**, not Stationary safety rules or a new authority/transaction/manager-release permission. Explicit operator **Stop**, actual profile switch, confirmed loss of Remote authority, mandatory SoC STOP and valid independent lifecycle/permission invalidation remain effective and must not be blocked for 10 ticks. These are **not** ordinary FAST/BALANCE downward fine-tuning. If conditions turn illegal for continued charging, do not hold the positive proposal merely to finish startup settling. |
| **PV-RESP-07** | **APPROVED 25 October follow-up:** the economic import-energy STOP at the configured **`pv_grid_import_budget_wh` (default 50 Wh)** overrides both the configurable startup hold and ordinary downward delay. Integrate eligible actual import on the existing PV ticks; when estimated accumulated Wh reaches the effective budget, request economic OFF on the first detecting cycle, without postponement until startup/reduction timers expire. Economic restart remains separately governed by PV-ECO-07/08. |
| **PV-RESP-08** | Acceptance: test 0→1p/6 A startup with 0%, 49.9%, exactly 50% and >50% measured nominal power, at ticks 1, 3, 9, 10 and 11. Verify ordinary downward hold for the full 10-cycle window and bounded downward release afterward; upward gating at 50% and three cycles; no artificial start from EV measured 0 W during an already positive offered 3p point. Simulate 30 s unlock charging drop and cell balancing underconsumption: no repeated upward steps, prompt justified downward decisions. Test an urgent operator STOP, SoC STOP and authority loss during startup: never delayed. Add phase-first 3p/6 A→1p/17 A and 1p/20 A→3p/7 A, 2p when advertised, disabled mode/current caps, large downward multi-step and phase-lockout reactions. Include exactly one relevant Level-2 log for each *changed* ramp/settling decision and none per held tick; see PV-DIAG-01–10. |

## Adjustable shared PV regulation parameters (25 October 2026)

These are **integration configuration parameters**, not Lovelace-card controls. They apply to both FAST and BALANCE unless explicitly FAST-only. Use the existing integration's normal Home Assistant configuration surface and validated settings, in **regulation cycles** rather than seconds for the three timing values. Exact technical identifiers are proposed for implementation; German UI names and defaults are the operator's requirement.

| ID | HA integration parameter (proposed key) | German UI name | Default | Units and behavior |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **PV-TUNE-01** | `pv_increase_interval_cycles` | Hochregelintervall | **3** | Positive integer **regulation cycles** (>=1). One upward **legal current step** at a time; no new increase before this interval, irrespective of FAST/BALANCE. |
| **PV-TUNE-02** | `pv_startup_hold_cycles` | Einschwingzeit nach Ladestart | **10** | Nonnegative integer **regulation cycles** (>=0). A true 0 A→positive start holds ordinary increases/reductions for this many eligible ticks. Zero disables only this additional optimization hold, not OCPP response/authority/SoC/economic checks. |
| **PV-TUNE-03** | `pv_grid_deadband_w` | Netzpunkt-Totzone | **100** | Nonnegative **watts** (>=0), **symmetric ±value** around 0 W signed grid exchange. Primarily FAST grid correction; not a global ban on upward exploration and not a substitute for economic grid accounting. The BALANCE grid economic fallback follows the same centralized grid-correction policy where used, not a competing second loop. |
| **PV-TUNE-04** | `pv_decrease_delay_cycles` | Runterregelverzögerung | **0** | Nonnegative integer **regulation cycles** (>=0). **0 = no additional wait:** if reduction is indicated on the current scheduled tick, act on this tick; no extra event-driven regulation loop. A positive N means the same material downward condition must persist for **N additional eligible regulation cycles after first detection** before ordinary downward optimization. Disappearing or reversing the condition clears the pending-delay count. |
| **PV-TUNE-05** | `pv_grid_import_budget_wh` | Zulässiger Netzbezug | **50** | Strictly positive **Wh** (>0). Shared economic import-energy **STOP trigger** for PV_SURPLUS with/without battery, PV_OPTIMUM and PV_MAXIMUM; replaces the historically fixed numeric 50 Wh while retaining the existing M/2 comparison, approximate integration, >15 s reset, authority/transaction accounting scope, STOP persistence and restart inhibition. |

**PV-TUNE-06 — Independent thresholds:** The parameter `pv_grid_deadband_w=100 W` affects ordinary signed-grid **setpoint correction** only. A 50 Wh budget is energy, not instantaneous power; the independent economic accounting gate remains `P_import > M/2` with the entire eligible positive imported power accumulated. Changing the deadband never changes economic integration or the import-energy STOP threshold.

**PV-TUNE-07 — Priority:** In every mode, explicit operator Stop, mandatory SoC STOP, authority/transaction fencing and the economic import-energy STOP are **not delayed** by the startup hold, downward delay, 50% EV uptake gate or upward interval. A changed target requiring a policy STOP is not an ordinary current decrement.

**PV-TUNE-08 — Boundaries and persistence:** The integration must validate finite, nonnegative whole-cycle parameters and the strictly positive import-energy budget. Values must persist, remain independent from `regulation_interval` (which stays in **seconds**) and be visible/editable through normal HA integration configuration. Provide explicit appropriate upper bounds in the implementation design rather than accepting unbounded values or guessing that zero means disabled for the Wh budget. Changing a setting must **not** trigger a second regulator or a fresh PV control cycle; the next ordinary tick uses effective settings. Use the same shared settings for all battery PV profiles.

**PV-TUNE-09 — Economic budget edits in active scope:** Historical accumulated Wh and economic STOP provenance survive integration reload and configuration changes; a threshold edit is **not** an accounting reset, transaction change or permission to restart. **Proposed implementation behavior pending final review:** lowering the budget below already accumulated eligible Wh triggers economic STOP on the next permitted ordinary cycle; increasing it does not cancel an already-issued STOP or clear restart inhibition. Persist the setting and keep accounting in actual Wh rather than a percentage of budget. Test active edits, restart, lower/raise and late ACKs.

**PV-TUNE-10 — Remove two obsolete parameters entirely:** The beta.16 preferences **`pv_start_delay` (default 0 seconds)** and **`pv_stop_delay` (default 90 seconds)** are unused by the active v0.4 PV runtime and are **not** retained as migration compatibility entities for the next PV milestone. Remove their HA Number entities, entity descriptions/translations, options/configuration editors, settings registration, stored keys and unused watchers. Do not repurpose their entity IDs for the new cycle-based parameters or silently interpret historical **seconds** as **cycles**. Existing historical `v0.3.2-beta.18` start-delay logic and the 90-second economic stop are superseded. Account for Home Assistant entity-registry/orphan cleanup without removing unrelated entities or user history.

### FAST ±100 W grid deadband [APPROVED configurable default]

Let `G = P_grid_import - P_grid_export` (+ import; − export). When `-pv_grid_deadband_w <= G <= pv_grid_deadband_w`, ignore minor grid-error **correction** rather than chasing noise. Do not suppress bounded FAST exploration when net grid exchange is near zero. Beyond the configured band, a genuine import can drive ordinary downward correction (subject to its cycle delay and startup hold) and export can support upward exploration (subject to response and ramp gates). Brief large cooking-load pulses may exceed the band; this alone does not authorize a second sampling loop or arbitrary additional smoothing. Replay pulses versus sustained demand with realistic EV/BYD response and the independent energy budget.
